I am a machine learning engineer at Microsoft in Seattle, where I have worked on production ranking,
retrieval, and agent systems since 2022. In September I start an M.S.E. in Robotics at the University
of Pennsylvania. I studied Computer Engineering at Georgia Tech.

Outside of work I run two research projects. The first asks whether self-supervised pretraining can
be made anatomy-aware for 3D retinal OCT, in collaboration with a PhD student at Columbia. The second
asks whether a frozen video world model can serve as a coarse robot manipulation controller, and
whether its own predictive energy is a usable signal for knowing when to hand off. Both are about the
same question in different clothes: what a learned representation actually knows, and how you would
tell.
