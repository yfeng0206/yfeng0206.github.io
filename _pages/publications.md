## Peer-Reviewed

**Evolution of Humanoid Locomotion Control**

Yan Gu, Guanya Shi, Fan Shi, I-Chia Chang, Yen-Jen Wang, Qilong Cheng, Zachary Olkin, Ivan Lopez-Sanchez, **Yunchu Feng**, Jian Zhang, Aaron D. Ames, Hao Su, Koushil Sreenath

*Science Robotics*, vol. 11, no. 117 (2026) &middot; with Prof. Hao Su, NYU &middot; May 2025 - Oct 2025

[doi:10.1126/scirobotics.aed3973](https://doi.org/10.1126/scirobotics.aed3973){: .btn .btn--info}

A review of humanoid locomotion control, tracing the field from model-based foundations through to
learning-based control. Covers balance control, motion planning, and optimization, spanning classical
algorithms such as MPC, LQR, and DCM alongside the reinforcement-learning and sim-to-real approaches
that now drive bipedal and humanoid locomotion. Contributing author, one of thirteen.
